# Looma — Textile Supply Chain Intelligence

Looma is an evidence-led platform for investigating textile certifications and manufacturers. It translates complex sustainability and labor standards into clear, decision-ready insights—showing not only what can be verified, but also what critical information is still missing.

## The problem

Sourcing teams often receive certificates, audit reports, and sustainability claims without an easy way to understand their scope, credibility, or limitations. Standards measure different things, supplier information is fragmented, and missing evidence can be difficult to spot.

## The solution

Looma creates a single investigation workspace where users can:

- Search textile certifications or manufacturers
- Upload supplier certification PDFs for review
- Compare standards side by side in plain language
- Evaluate environmental, labor, and traceability evidence
- Visualize a manufacturer’s supply chain from farm to factory
- Identify unverifiable claims and missing documentation
- Ask contextual follow-up questions and generate supplier requests

## Product principles

- **Evidence over marketing** — conclusions are tied to visible sources.
- **Gaps are findings** — missing or outdated evidence is surfaced explicitly.
- **Complexity without jargon** — standards are translated into practical sourcing decisions.
- **Confidence is transparent** — verified, partial, and unsupported claims remain distinct.

## Current version

This repository contains an interactive product prototype with representative investigation data. Production development would connect certification registries, supply-chain datasets, document extraction, and manufacturer due-diligence sources.
